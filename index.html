About Quantum Academy

Quantum Academy is an innovative educational organization based in Karachi, Pakistan, dedicated to empowering students with practical skills in Robotics, Artificial Intelligence (AI), STEM education, and emerging technologies.

Founded in 2021 as Mr Robotics. Operating as Quantum Academy since 2024.

Vision

To become a leading provider of Robotics, AI, and STEM education, nurturing a generation of creative thinkers and technology innovators.

Mission

To deliver quality, practical, and future-focused technology education that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to succeed in an increasingly technology-driven world.
